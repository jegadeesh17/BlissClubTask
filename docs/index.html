Critical Vulnerability #1

Over-Reliance on Aggregator A3 & Courier C9

Aggregator A3 handles 73.0% of total company volume (67,642 orders) and consumes ₹50.25 Lakhs of freight spend. Within A3, Courier C9 alone ships 62.4% of all orders.

The Financial Impact: Across Zones A, B, C, and E, A3 is your most expensive contracted partner (₹24 in Zone A vs A1's ₹19; ₹56 in Zone E vs A1's ₹41). We are paying an 11.8% premium to route volume to A3, leaving ₹71.4 Lakhs/year on the table.
The Actionable Operational Fix:

Phase a 25% reallocation of Zone A and Zone B volume to Aggregators A1 and A2. This immediately frees ₹55,864/month in pure profit without exceeding partner capacity thresholds.

Operational Finding #2

Cash-on-Delivery (COD) Drives 89.9% of All Returns

Prepaid orders demonstrate an extraordinary 99.16% delivery rate (0.80% RTO). In stark contrast, COD orders experience an RTO rate of 12.23% (4,163 returns).

The Customer Experience Reality: Return-to-Origin is fundamentally a checkout payment friction and buyer commitment problem, not a courier in-transit speed issue. Every COD return incurs forward freight, reverse freight, and inventory locking.
The Actionable Operational Fix:

Nudge online prepayment with instant ₹30–₹50 UPI discounts at checkout, and enforce automated WhatsApp OTP/address confirmation on Tier 2/3 COD orders after attempt #1.

Q5 · Part 2 of 3

The First-Mile Handover Bottleneck

Parcels sit manifested on dispatch docks for an average of 16 hours awaiting courier pickup.

Root Cause Analysis

Why Click-to-Deliver Speed is 3.17 Days (76 Hours)

Warehouse staff pack orders in under 1 hour during the day. However, couriers do not perform pickup scans until late evening or the next morning.

The Finding: Over 55% of all orders sit manifested on the warehouse dock for more than 12 hours. Eliminating this dock lag by enforcing daily 18:30 courier pickup cut-offs immediately cuts 12 to 18 hours off overall Click-to-Deliver speed without altering linehaul networks.

Q5 · Part 3 of 3

Enforceable SLA Contract Structure

Holding warehouse teams and courier partners accountable through contractual penalties.

1. Warehouse Dispatch Cutoff

Internal WMS SLA: ≤ 12 Hours

Orders placed before 14:00 must be packed and manifested by 16:00. Shift leads are tracked on manifest turnaround.

2. Courier Pickup Window

Handover SLA: ≤ 4 Hours

Couriers must scan cages by 19:00 same-day. Contract penalty: ₹10 per parcel billing credit on missed pickup windows.

3. Transit Zone SLAs

Linehaul SLA: 24h to 120h

Zone A: 24h | Zone B: 48h | Zone C/D: 72–96h. Contract penalty: 25% freight credit on parcels exceeding SLA by >24h.

Weight Slabs & Formulas

Billable Weight Logic

Charges are billed per AWB based on 500g increments. Weights are rounded UP to the nearest 0.5 kg slab: Billable Slabs = ceil(Weight / 0.5). Total cost = Base Rate + (Additional Slabs × Additional Rate) + COD Fee.
